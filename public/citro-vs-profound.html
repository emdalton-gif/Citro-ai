Where Profound is the better choice

  • You want scheduled tracking at scale. Profound tracks your prompts on a schedule and is built for large prompt sets.
  • ChatGPT is the only assistant you care about. Starter covers it at $99 a month if you are happy to pay for the year up front.
  • You have an enterprise team. Profound's Enterprise plan covers up to nine engines with custom reporting.

Where Citro is the better choice

  • You want every major assistant, not just ChatGPT. Your buyers use Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Grok too. Citro runs all five on every audit at one price.
  • You don't want an annual commitment. Citro is billed monthly, cancel anytime, with a 7-day free trial.
  • You need to know what to do next. Citro shows who AI names instead of you and which sites those answers read, checks your site, and gives you a 90-day plan that won't tell you to build what you already have.

The bottom line

Profound is a strong analytics platform, especially for ChatGPT tracking and for large teams. If you are a small team that wants all five assistants, monthly billing, and a plan you can act on this week, start with Citro.

Common questions

Is Profound cheaper than Citro?
Profound's Starter plan is $99 a month but billed annually ($1,188 up front) and covers ChatGPT only. Its Growth plan is $399 a month, also annual. Citro is $119 a month, billed monthly, and covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Grok.
Does Citro track my brand automatically like Profound?
Citro runs on demand rather than on a schedule. You can re-run as often as you like, and your score history is kept so you can see it change. If you need automatic daily tracking, Profound and other trackers do that.
What AI assistants does Citro test?
Every Citro audit asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Claude and Grok, live with web search, so the answers reflect what each assistant finds today.
Can I use Citro before committing to a bigger platform?
Yes. Many teams start with the free check or the 7-day trial to see the gaps and build the case internally before buying an annual contract anywhere.
