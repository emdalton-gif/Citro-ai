Where Peec AI is the better choice

  • You sell in several countries or languages. Peec tracks answers in more than 14 languages with country breakdowns. Citro audits US answers.
  • You care how AI describes you, not just whether it names you. Peec's sentiment scoring is one of its strongest features.
  • You want a daily chart. Peec runs your prompts every day. Citro runs when you ask it to.
  • Google AI Overviews or Copilot matter most. Peec lets you pick them as engines. Citro does not cover them.

Where Citro is the better choice

  • You want every major assistant without picking three. Citro runs ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Grok on every audit at one price. On Peec, Claude is Enterprise-only and extra engines cost more each month.
  • You are not showing up yet. Daily tracking of a zero doesn't tell you what to fix. Citro shows who gets named instead of you, which sites those answers read, and what is missing on your own site.
  • You want a plan a small team can work through. Citro's 90-day plan names the pages to build or update and the sites to get listed on, and never recommends something your site already has.
  • You want the questions your buyers ask. Citro builds buyer personas for your market and writes the questions they would put to AI. You can edit both.

Using both

Plenty of teams pair a daily tracker with a periodic audit. Track the trend with Peec AI, and run Citro when the numbers stall to find out why and to set the next 90 days of work. If you can only start with one and you are not yet appearing in AI answers, start with the diagnosis.

The bottom line

Peec AI is a strong tracker, especially for sentiment and multi-language brands. Citro is the audit for small teams that want all five assistants, the reasons they are missing, and a plan to fix it.

Common questions

Is Citro cheaper than Peec AI?
Yes, to start. Peec AI's Starter plan is about $95 a month (€89) for 50 prompts tracked daily on three engines, with extra engines about $35 a month each. Citro Starter is $29 a month for a full monthly audit on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Grok, a site check and a 90-day plan. Citro Professional, at $119 a month, adds unlimited re-runs.
Does Peec AI include Claude?
As of October 2026, Claude is available on Peec AI's Enterprise plans only. Self-serve plans let you choose three engines from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot.
Does Citro track daily like Peec AI?
No. Citro runs on demand, as often as you like, and keeps your score history. If you need automatic daily tracking or sentiment in many languages, Peec AI does that well.
Can I try Citro for free?
Yes. The free check asks ChatGPT and Gemini three buyer questions with live web search, with no card. The full audit starts at $29 a month.
