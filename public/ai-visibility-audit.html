What an AI visibility audit is

An AI visibility audit measures whether AI assistants recommend you when a buyer asks a question like "who is the best accountant near me" or "what software should a small clinic use." It runs those questions across the assistants your buyers use and records three things: whether you were named, who was named instead, and which websites the answer drew on.

The last part matters most. AI assistants build their answers from pages they find on the web: review sites, directories, "best of" lists, Reddit threads and your competitors' own pages. If you are missing from those sources, or your own site is hard for AI to read, you get left out. A useful audit shows you exactly which of those gaps is costing you.

How to run an AI visibility audit yourself

You can do a basic audit by hand in an afternoon. Here is the process.

  1. Write 5 to 10 buyer questions. Use the words a customer would use, not your industry's jargon. "Who does emergency plumbing in Grand Rapids" beats "residential plumbing services provider."
  2. Ask each assistant with web search on. Put every question to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Grok. Use a logged-out or fresh session so past chats don't skew the answer, and don't mention your business name.
  3. Record who gets named. For each answer, note whether you appear, where you appear in the list, and which competitors appear.
  4. Record the sources. Most assistants cite the pages they used. List them. The sites that show up again and again are where you need to be listed or mentioned.
  5. Check your own site. Make sure your robots.txt doesn't block AI crawlers such as GPTBot and PerplexityBot, add an llms.txt file, add schema markup for your business and services, and make sure each service you sell has its own clear page.
  6. Pick three fixes and repeat in a month. Get listed on the sources that keep coming up, fill the gaps on your site, then ask the same questions again to see what changed.

The hard part is doing it the same way every time. Answers change from one session to the next, so a one-off manual check can mislead you. That is the reason to use a tool.
