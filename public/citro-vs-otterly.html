Citro vs. Otterly.AI

Otterly.AI and Citro both show whether AI assistants recommend your brand, but they answer different questions. Otterly tracks a set of prompts every day and charts how often you are mentioned. Citro asks five assistants the questions your buyers ask, shows who gets named instead of you and which sites those answers read, checks your own site, and gives you a 90-day plan. Where Otterly.AI is the better choice

  • You want a daily chart. Otterly runs your prompts every day on its own. Citro runs when you ask it to.
  • Google AI Overviews and Copilot matter to you. Both are in Otterly's base plan. Citro does not cover them.
  • You want daily tracking at the entry price. Otterly Lite and Citro Starter are both $29 a month. Otterly tracks 15 prompts every day; Citro runs a full audit once a month.
  • You track hundreds of prompts. Otterly's higher tiers are built for large prompt sets and agencies running many clients.

Where Citro is the better choice

  • You are not showing up at all. A chart of zero mentions does not tell you what to do. Citro shows who AI names instead of you, which sites those answers read, and what to change, in order.
  • You want Claude and Grok without add-ons. All five assistants run on every Citro audit at one price. On Otterly, Gemini and Claude cost extra and Grok is not listed.
  • You want the questions your buyers ask, not keywords. Citro builds personas for your market and writes the questions they would ask. You can edit both.
  • You have no SEO analyst. The plan is written for a small team: specific pages to build or update, sites to get listed on, and what to fix on your own site, with nothing recommended that your site already has.

Using both

The two tools don't exclude each other. Some teams keep a daily tracker running and use Citro once a month to find out why the numbers look the way they do and to set the next 90 days of work. If you are choosing one and you are not yet appearing in AI answers, start with the diagnosis. If you already appear and want to watch the trend every day, start with tracking.

The bottom line

Otterly.AI is a solid, low-cost tracker. Citro is the audit you run when tracking shows a problem, or before you start tracking at all: five assistants, your buyers' questions, a check of your site, and a 90-day plan.

Common questions

How does Citro's price compare with Otterly.AI?
They start at the same price. Otterly.AI is $29 a month for 15 prompts tracked daily on four engines, with Gemini and Claude as paid add-ons. Citro Starter is $29 a month for a full monthly audit on all five assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Grok), a site check and a 90-day plan. Citro Professional, at $119 a month, adds unlimited re-runs.
Does Citro track my brand every day like Otterly.AI?
No. Citro runs on demand, as often as you like, and keeps your score history so you can see it move. If you need automatic daily tracking, Otterly.AI does that.
Does Otterly.AI include Claude and Gemini?
As of October 2026, Gemini, Google AI Mode and Claude are paid add-ons on every Otterly.AI plan. Its base plans include ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot.
Can I try Citro for free?
Yes. The free check needs no card and asks ChatGPT and Gemini three buyer questions with live web search. The full audit starts at $29 a month.
