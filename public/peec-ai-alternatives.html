Why people look for a Peec AI alternative

  • Price. About $95 a month to start, plus roughly $35 a month for each extra engine on Starter, is a lot for a small business that just wants to know where it stands.
  • Engine limits. You pick three engines on the entry plan, and Claude is Enterprise-only.
  • Tracking without a diagnosis. A daily chart is useful once you appear. If the chart reads zero every day, it doesn't tell you what to fix.

The six alternatives

1. Citro: best for finding out why you're missing

Citro is an AI visibility audit for small teams. Every run asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Grok the questions your buyers ask, with live web search. You see who gets named instead of you, which sites each answer read, what on your own site is holding you back, and a 90-day plan to fix it. Starter is $29 a month for one full audit a month, and Professional is $119 a month for unlimited re-runs.

Trade-off: Citro runs on demand rather than daily, covers US English answers, and doesn't track Google AI Overviews or Copilot. See the full Citro vs Peec AI comparison.

2. Otterly.AI: best for low-cost daily tracking

Otterly.AI is the lowest-priced daily tracker, at $29 a month for 15 prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Copilot. It also has a page-level GEO audit.

Trade-off: Gemini, Google AI Mode and Claude cost extra, and 15 prompts goes quickly. See Citro vs Otterly.AI.

3. Profound: best for large brands

Profound offers deep analytics and, on Enterprise, coverage of up to nine engines. It is built for marketing teams at larger companies.

Trade-off: The $99 Starter plan covers ChatGPT only and is billed annually. See Citro vs Profound.

4. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit: best if you already use Semrush

If your team already works in Semrush, its AI toolkit adds AI visibility tracking next to your SEO data for $99 a month with 25 prompts.

Trade-off: It's an add-on to a larger suite, so it makes the most sense if you're already paying for Semrush.

5. Ahrefs Brand Radar: best if you already use Ahrefs

Brand Radar uses Ahrefs' data to show brand mentions across AI assistants, starting at $199 a month per index.

Trade-off: It's priced for teams already invested in Ahrefs.

6. AthenaHQ: best for wide engine coverage

AthenaHQ tracks a broad list of engines and regions, with a free tier and paid plans metered by credits.

Trade-off: Paid plans start at $295 a month, well above Peec AI.

When to stay with Peec AI

Stay with Peec AI if you sell in several countries or languages, if sentiment (how AI describes you, not just whether it names you) matters most, or if you want Google AI Overviews and Copilot on a daily chart. It does those things well.
